# Remote Work and Expenses Policy

## Section 1.1 Remote Work Eligibility
After probation, employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head.

## Section 1.2 Work From Home Setup Stipend
Employees approved for remote work receive a one-time setup stipend of Rs 25,000 for a desk, chair, or monitor. The claim must be filed within 60 days of starting remote work.

## Section 2.1 Internet Reimbursement
The company reimburses internet costs up to Rs 2,000 per month. The bill must be submitted by the 25th of the month.

## Section 3.1 Travel Approval
Any business trip expected to cost more than Rs 10,000 needs written approval from the manager before booking. Travel is economy class.

## Section 3.2 Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, with receipts attached, through the finance portal.
