# IT Security and Access Policy

## Section 1.1 VPN Access Requests
To get VPN access, raise a ticket in the IT portal under the category "Network Access" and include your manager's approval. VPN access is set up within 4 business hours of approval.

## Section 1.2 VPN Usage
The VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems from outside the office network.

## Section 2.1 Password Rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, are rotated every 90 days, and cannot repeat any of the last 5 passwords.

## Section 2.2 Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory on all company accounts and must use an authenticator app. SMS-based codes are not accepted.

## Section 3.1 Faulty Laptop Replacement
A faulty company laptop is replaced within 24 hours for in-office employees. Remote employees receive a replacement within 3 business days by courier.

## Section 3.2 Lost or Stolen Devices
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 2 hours of discovery.

## Section 4.1 Software Requests
Software on the approved list is installed within 2 business days of a request. Software not on the approved list needs a security review, which takes up to 5 business days.
