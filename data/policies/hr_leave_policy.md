# HR Leave Policy

## Section 1.1 Annual Leave
Every full-time employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.5 days per month.

## Section 1.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

## Section 1.3 Casual Leave
Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 2 consecutive days.

## Section 2.1 Carryover
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 and are not paid out.

## Section 2.2 Advance Notice
Leave of 1 to 3 days needs 2 working days of notice. Leave of 4 to 5 days needs 7 days of notice. Leave of more than 5 consecutive days needs 14 days of advance notice and written manager approval.

## Section 3.1 Unpaid Leave
If an employee has no leave balance left, additional days are unpaid (loss of pay) and need approval from both the manager and HR.

## Section 3.2 How to Apply
All leave requests are submitted through the HR portal under "My Leave". The manager approves or rejects the request in the same portal.
