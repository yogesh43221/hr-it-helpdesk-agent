# Onboarding and Payroll Policy

## Section 1.1 Probation
New employees serve a 3-month probation period. The notice period during probation is 15 days.

## Section 1.2 Onboarding Checklist
On day one, employees receive their laptop and company accounts. HR collects ID proof, education documents, and bank details within the first week.

## Section 2.1 Salary Payment Date
Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.

## Section 2.2 Payslips
Payslips are available on the HR portal on the 1st of the following month.

## Section 3.1 Tax Declarations
Investment and tax-saving declarations must be submitted by January 31 each year through the HR portal.

## Section 3.2 Changing Bank Details
To change bank details, submit a request in the HR portal with a cancelled cheque. Requests received before the 20th take effect in that month's salary. Later requests apply from the next month.
